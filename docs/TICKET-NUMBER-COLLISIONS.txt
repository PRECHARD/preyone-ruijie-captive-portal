TICKET NUMBER COLLISION REPORT
Generated for office reconciliation - legacy untagged tickets only.
Snapshot: 2026-09-29, production (captive_portal / Mupota Bus Service)

WHAT THIS IS
  Before tagged numbers existed, a ticket number was a per-phone counter with
  no device component, so every handset started at AGJ0001. Where two buses
  sold the same sequence, two different passengers were given the same printed
  number. These are REAL paid sales, not sync replays.

  364 ticket rows carry only 284 distinct numbers. 80 rows are surplus.
  19 numbers are affected. 4 handsets are involved.

  New tickets are fixed (see the tagged format and the unique index on
  (company_id, client_receipt_no) for tagged numbers only). These legacy rows
  were deliberately LEFT ALONE: demoting them would hide real revenue and
  would not change the paper already in passengers' hands.

HOW TO USE THIS
  KEEP   = the oldest sale for that number. Count it normally.
  SURPLUS= a later real sale that happens to share an earlier number. It is a
           genuine sale and must NOT be deleted or voided - it needs manual
           cross-reference against the KEEP row (same date/trip/amount) to
           decide which passenger each paper ticket belongs to.
  Trips listed at the end are over-counted in seats_sold because that figure
  counts rows rather than distinct numbers.
